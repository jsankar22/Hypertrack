# Training Splits and Programming Principles

## Core principles
- Progressive overload: gradually do more over time by adding reps, then load (double progression), or adding sets.
- Volume: about 10-20 hard sets per muscle group per week suits most people for muscle growth. Beginners often grow well on fewer sets.
- Frequency: training each muscle at least twice per week tends to work better than once, mainly because it makes it easier to spread volume across sessions.
- Intensity: take most sets to within 1-3 repetitions of failure. Repetition ranges of roughly 5-30 can all build muscle if sets are hard enough; 6-12 is a practical default, and 1-6 reps is typical for strength work.
- Rest between sets: 2-3 minutes for heavy compound lifts, 1-2 minutes for isolation work.
- Technique first: use a full range of motion with controlled repetitions and add weight only when form stays consistent.

## Full body (3 days per week)
Best for beginners and anyone with limited time. Train Monday, Wednesday, Friday.
- Squat or leg press 3x6-10
- Bench press or dips 3x6-10
- Barbell row or pull-up 3x6-10
- Overhead press 2-3x8-12
- Romanian deadlift 2x8-10, plus optional arms and calves

## Upper / Lower (4 days per week)
Good balance of frequency and recovery. Example: Monday upper, Tuesday lower, Thursday upper, Friday lower.
- Upper: bench press 4x6-8, barbell row 4x6-8, overhead press 3x8-10, pull-up 3x6-10, lateral raise 3x12-15, arm work 2-3 sets each
- Lower: squat 4x5-8, Romanian deadlift 3x8-10, leg press 3x10-12, leg curl 3x10-12, calf raise 3x10-15

## Push / Pull / Legs (3 or 6 days per week)
Run once per week on 3 days, or twice per week on 6 days for more volume. It suits intermediates who want more exercise variety per muscle.
- Push: bench press 4x6-8, overhead press 3x8-10, dips 3x8-12, lateral raise 3x12-15, triceps pushdown 3x10-12
- Pull: pull-up 4x6-10, barbell row 4x6-8, face pull 3x12-15, biceps curl 3x10-12, optional deadlift 1-3x3-5
- Legs: squat 4x6-8, Romanian deadlift 3x8-10, leg press 3x10-12, leg curl 3x10-12, calf raise 4x10-15

## Choosing a split
- 2-3 days available: full body.
- 4 days available: upper/lower.
- 5 days available: push/pull/legs plus upper/lower, or upper/lower/push/pull/legs.
- 6 days available: push/pull/legs twice per week; only if recovery, sleep and nutrition are good.
The best split is the one the person can follow consistently. Adherence matters more than the exact template.

## Managing progress
Log every session (exercise, weight, sets, reps). If lifts stall for 3 or more weeks despite good sleep and food, review volume, calories and technique, then consider a deload. Change exercises only when needed; keep the main lifts for at least 8-12 weeks.
